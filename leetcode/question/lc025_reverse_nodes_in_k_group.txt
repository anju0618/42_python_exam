Based on: LeetCode 25. Reverse Nodes in k-Group

Assignment (paraphrased)
Given the head of a linked list, reverse the nodes k at a time and
return the new head. k is positive and at most the list length. If the
remaining nodes at the end are fewer than k, leave them as they are.
Only node links may change, not values.
