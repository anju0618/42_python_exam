lc875_koko_eating_bananas
Based on: LeetCode 875. Koko Eating Bananas

Assignment (paraphrased)
There are piles of bananas; pile i has piles[i] bananas. Koko eats at a
constant speed of k bananas per hour: each hour she picks one pile and
eats k from it (or the whole pile if it has fewer than k left, and does
not move to another pile that same hour). She must finish every pile
within h hours. Return the minimum integer k that lets her finish in
time.

Output

Output
30
