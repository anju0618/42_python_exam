py_multiply_strings  [Hard]

Assignment
`a` and `b` are non-negative integers written as strings (no leading
zeros except "0" itself). Return their product as a string.
Do NOT convert the whole strings with int() and do not use big-number
tricks: do the schoolbook multiplication on digits.

Function signature
def multiply_strings(a: str, b: str) -> str:

Examples
Input
multiply_strings("2", "3")
Output
"6"
Input
multiply_strings("123", "456")
Output
"56088"
