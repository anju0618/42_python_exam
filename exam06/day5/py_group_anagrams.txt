py_group_anagrams  [Medium]

Assignment
Group the words that are anagrams of each other (same letters, same
counts). Groups appear in the order of their first word in `words`, and
words inside a group keep their input order.

Function signature
def group_anagrams(words: list[str]) -> list[list[str]]:

Examples
Input
group_anagrams(["eat","tea","tan","ate","nat","bat"])
Output
[["eat", "tea", "ate"], ["tan", "nat"], ["bat"]]
Input
group_anagrams([""])
Output
[[""]]

---------- 和訳 ----------
課題
互いにアナグラム(同じ文字・同じ個数)になっている単語をグループにまとめる。
グループは「最初の単語が現れた順」、グループ内は入力順を保つ。
